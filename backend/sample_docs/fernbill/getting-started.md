# Getting started with Fernbill

Fernbill is invoicing software for freelancers and small businesses. This guide walks you through your first fifteen minutes.

## Create your account

Sign up at fernbill.example with your email address or a Google account. You get a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No card is needed to start the trial.

## Set up your business profile

Go to **Settings > Business profile** and add:

- Your business name and address, as they should appear on invoices
- Your logo (PNG or JPG, up to 2 MB)
- Your tax number, if you have one
- Your default currency

You can change all of these later. Invoices that were already sent keep the details they were sent with.

## Add your first client

Open **Clients > New client**. Only a name and an email address are required. Add a billing address if your client needs it on the invoice.

## Send your first invoice

Go to **Invoices > New invoice**, choose the client, add line items and click **Send**. Your client receives an email with a link to view and pay the invoice online. See "Creating and sending invoices" for details.

## Where to get help

Search this help center first. If you can't find an answer, email support@fernbill.example. Support replies Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. UK time, usually within one business day.
