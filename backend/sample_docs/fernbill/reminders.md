# Automatic payment reminders

Payment reminders are available on the Growth and Business plans. They email clients about unpaid invoices so you don't have to chase them yourself.

## Turn on reminders

Go to **Settings > Reminders** and turn on **Send automatic reminders**. The default schedule is:

- 3 days before the due date
- On the due date
- 7 days after the due date
- 14 days after the due date

You can add, remove or change reminders. The maximum is 6 reminders per invoice.

## Edit the reminder email

Each reminder uses a template you can edit. You can include placeholders such as the client's name, the invoice number, the amount due and the payment link.

## Late fees

You can add a late fee to overdue invoices in **Settings > Reminders > Late fees**. Choose a fixed amount or a percentage of the unpaid balance, and how many days after the due date it applies. Fernbill adds the fee once. It doesn't compound.

## Turn off reminders for one client

Open the client's page and turn off **Send reminders to this client**. This is useful for clients you've agreed different terms with.
