# Team members and roles

You can invite people to your Fernbill account. The number of users depends on your plan: 1 on Starter, up to 3 on Growth and up to 15 on Business.

## Invite a team member

Go to **Settings > Team > Invite**. Enter their email and choose a role. The invitation link expires after 7 days. You can resend it from the same page.

## Roles

- **Owner:** full access, including billing and deleting the account. Each account has exactly one owner.
- **Admin:** full access except billing and deleting the account.
- **Accountant:** can view everything, record payments and export data, but can't send invoices or change settings.
- **Staff:** can create and send invoices and manage clients, but can't see reports or settings.

Custom roles and per-client permissions are only available on the Business plan.

## Transfer ownership

The current owner can transfer ownership to an Admin in **Settings > Team**. The new owner must accept by email. After the transfer, the previous owner becomes an Admin.

## Remove a team member

Open **Settings > Team**, choose the person and click **Remove**. Their access ends immediately. Invoices they created stay in the account.
