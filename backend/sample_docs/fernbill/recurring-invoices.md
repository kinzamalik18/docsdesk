# Recurring invoices

Recurring invoices are useful for retainers, subscriptions and any work you bill on a regular schedule. They are available on the Growth and Business plans.

## Set up a recurring invoice

1. Create an invoice as usual.
2. Before sending, open **Schedule** and turn on **Repeat this invoice**.
3. Choose how often it repeats: weekly, every two weeks, monthly, quarterly or yearly.
4. Choose an end condition: after a number of invoices, on a date, or never.

Fernbill creates and sends each new invoice at 8 a.m. in your account's time zone on the scheduled day.

## Automatic charging

If your client saved a card when paying an earlier invoice, you can turn on **Charge automatically**. Fernbill then charges the saved card on the invoice date and emails your client a receipt. If the charge fails, Fernbill retries after 3 days and again after 7 days, then marks the invoice as overdue.

## Pause or stop a schedule

Open **Invoices > Recurring**, choose the schedule and click **Pause** or **Stop**. Pausing keeps the schedule so you can resume it later. Stopping ends it permanently. Invoices that were already sent are not affected.
