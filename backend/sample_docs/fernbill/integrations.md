# Integrations

## QuickBooks Online and Xero

On the Business plan, you can connect QuickBooks Online or Xero in **Settings > Integrations**. Only one accounting integration can be active at a time.

Once connected, Fernbill syncs these items every hour:

- New clients, as customers or contacts
- Sent invoices, including line items and tax
- Payments recorded in Fernbill

The sync goes one way, from Fernbill to your accounting software. Changes you make in QuickBooks or Xero are not copied back to Fernbill.

## Zapier

All plans can connect to Zapier. Fernbill triggers include "Invoice sent", "Invoice paid" and "New client". You can use these to post to Slack, add rows to a spreadsheet and more.

## API

The Fernbill API is available on the Business plan. Create an API key in **Settings > Developers**. Keys have full access to the account, so store them safely. The rate limit is 120 requests per minute per key.

## Disconnecting an integration

Disconnecting stops future syncs. Records that already synced stay in your accounting software.
