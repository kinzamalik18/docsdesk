# My client didn't receive the invoice email

If a client says they didn't get your invoice, work through these checks.

## 1. Check the invoice activity

Open the invoice and look at **Activity**. It shows when the email was sent, delivered and opened. If it says "Bounced", the client's email server rejected the message. Check the email address for typos and send again.

## 2. Ask the client to check spam

Invoice emails come from invoices@fernbill.example. Ask your client to check their spam or junk folder and to add this address to their contacts.

## 3. Send the link another way

On the invoice page, click **Copy link**. You can send the link through any channel, such as a chat app or your own email. The link works the same way as the one in the email.

## 4. Use your own domain

On the Business plan, you can send invoices from your own domain, such as billing@yourcompany.com. This usually improves delivery. Go to **Settings > Email > Custom domain** and add the DNS records shown there. Verification usually takes less than an hour but can take up to 48 hours.

## Still not working?

Email support@fernbill.example with the invoice number and the client's email address, and we'll check the delivery logs.
