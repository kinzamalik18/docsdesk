# Creating and sending invoices

## Create an invoice

Go to **Invoices > New invoice** and choose a client. Add line items with a description, quantity and unit price. Fernbill calculates subtotals, tax and the total automatically.

Invoice numbers are assigned in sequence, starting from INV-0001. To change the format or the starting number, go to **Settings > Invoice numbering**. You can't reuse a number that was already used on a sent invoice.

## Due dates and payment terms

The default payment term is 30 days. You can set a different default in **Settings > Payment terms** or change it on a single invoice. Common options are Due on receipt, Net 7, Net 14, Net 30 and Net 60.

## Send an invoice

Click **Send** to email the invoice to your client. The email includes a secure link to view, download and pay the invoice. You can also download a PDF and send it yourself.

## Edit a sent invoice

You can edit a sent invoice until it receives any payment. Your client always sees the latest version through the link. Once an invoice is partly or fully paid, it is locked. To correct a paid invoice, issue a credit note from the invoice page.

## Delete or void an invoice

Draft invoices can be deleted. Sent invoices can't be deleted, but you can mark them as void. Voided invoices stay in your records for accounting purposes and are excluded from reports.
