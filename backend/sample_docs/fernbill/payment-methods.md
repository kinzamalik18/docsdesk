# Getting paid online

Fernbill lets your clients pay invoices online by card or bank transfer. Payments go through Stripe or PayPal, which you connect in **Settings > Payments**.

## Connect Stripe

Click **Connect Stripe** and follow the steps to sign in or create a Stripe account. Once connected, clients can pay by credit or debit card, Apple Pay and Google Pay. In supported countries, they can also pay by bank debit.

## Connect PayPal

Click **Connect PayPal** and sign in to your PayPal Business account. Personal PayPal accounts can't be connected.

## Fees

Fernbill doesn't charge any fee on payments. Stripe and PayPal charge their own processing fees, which are deducted before the money reaches you. You can choose to add a surcharge to card payments in **Settings > Payments > Surcharges**, where local law allows it.

## When money arrives

Payouts follow your Stripe or PayPal payout schedule. Fernbill marks the invoice as paid as soon as the payment succeeds, even if the payout reaches your bank later.

## Offline payments

If a client pays by cash, cheque or a bank transfer outside Fernbill, open the invoice and click **Record payment**. Enter the amount, date and method. The invoice updates to partly paid or paid.
