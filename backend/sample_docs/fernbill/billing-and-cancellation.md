# Changing your plan, billing and cancellation

## Upgrade or downgrade

Go to **Settings > Billing > Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately, and you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

If you downgrade to Starter with more than 25 active clients, you keep all your clients but can't create invoices for new ones until you archive enough to get under the limit.

## Payment for your subscription

Fernbill accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express for subscriptions. Business plan customers on annual billing can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice.

## Download receipts

All subscription receipts are in **Settings > Billing > Receipts**. You can add a VAT number or a different billing address there, and new receipts will include it.

## Cancel your subscription

Go to **Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription**. Your paid plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then your account moves to the free read-only mode. In read-only mode you can view and download your invoices but can't send new ones.

## Refunds

Monthly subscriptions are not refunded for partial months. Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the first payment or the most recent renewal. After 14 days, annual plans are not refunded, but you keep access until the end of the year. To request a refund, email support@fernbill.example from the account owner's email address.
