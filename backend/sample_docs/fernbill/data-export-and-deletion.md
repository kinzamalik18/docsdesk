# Exporting and deleting your data

## Export your data

Go to **Settings > Data > Export**. You can export:

- Clients as CSV
- Invoices and payments as CSV
- All invoice PDFs as a ZIP file

Large exports are prepared in the background. Fernbill emails you a download link when the export is ready. The link is valid for 48 hours.

Exports are available on every plan, including read-only mode after cancellation.

## Delete your account

Only the account owner can delete the account. Go to **Settings > Data > Delete account** and confirm with your password.

Deleting an account is permanent. After you confirm, you have a 30-day grace period: if you sign in during that time, you can restore the account. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted and can't be recovered.

Please export your data before deleting your account. Depending on your country, you may be legally required to keep invoice records for several years.

## Data retention after cancellation

If you cancel but don't delete your account, your data stays in read-only mode for as long as you keep the account. Fernbill doesn't delete inactive read-only accounts.
