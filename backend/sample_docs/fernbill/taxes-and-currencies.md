# Taxes and currencies

## Add tax rates

Go to **Settings > Taxes** and click **Add tax rate**. Give it a name, such as VAT or GST, and a percentage. You can add up to two tax rates to each line item, which covers cases like compound sales taxes.

Set a default tax rate to have it applied to new line items automatically. You can still remove it from any line.

## Tax-inclusive prices

By default, prices are tax-exclusive and tax is added on top. To enter prices that already include tax, turn on **Prices include tax** in **Settings > Taxes**. This setting applies to new invoices only.

## Multi-currency invoicing

On the Growth and Business plans, you can invoice in over 130 currencies. Choose the currency when creating an invoice, or set a default currency for each client.

Reports show amounts in your account's default currency. Fernbill converts other currencies using the exchange rate on the date of each invoice. Rates come from the European Central Bank and are updated once a day.

## Tax advice

Fernbill can't tell you which taxes to charge. Rules depend on your country and your client's country. Please check with an accountant or your local tax authority.
