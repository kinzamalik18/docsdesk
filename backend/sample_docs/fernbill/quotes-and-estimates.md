# Quotes and estimates

Send a quote before you start work, and turn it into an invoice when your client accepts. Quotes are available on all plans.

## Create a quote

Go to **Quotes > New quote**. Add line items as you would on an invoice, and set an expiry date. The default expiry is 30 days.

## How clients accept

Your client receives an email with a link to the quote. They can click **Accept** or **Decline**, and can add a comment. You get a notification either way. On the Business plan, you can require a typed signature to accept.

## Deposits

You can ask for a deposit when the client accepts. Set it as a percentage or a fixed amount in the quote's **Deposit** section. Fernbill creates a deposit invoice automatically when the quote is accepted.

## Convert a quote to an invoice

Open an accepted quote and click **Convert to invoice**. All line items are copied. If a deposit was paid, it is deducted from the final invoice.

## Expired quotes

Expired quotes can't be accepted. To extend one, open it and change the expiry date, then send it again.
