# Plans and pricing

Fernbill has three plans. Prices are per month, billed monthly, and exclude VAT where it applies. Annual billing saves two months: you pay for 10 months and get 12.

## Starter: $9 per month

- Up to 25 active clients
- Unlimited invoices and quotes
- Stripe and PayPal payments
- 1 user

## Growth: $24 per month

- Unlimited clients
- Recurring invoices and automatic charging
- Automatic payment reminders
- Multi-currency invoicing
- Up to 3 users

## Business: $59 per month

- Everything in Growth
- Up to 15 users with roles and permissions
- QuickBooks and Xero integrations
- Priority email support with a 4-hour response target during business hours
- Custom invoice domain

## Free trial

New accounts get a 14-day trial of the Growth plan. When the trial ends, your account moves to the Starter plan unless you choose a paid plan. You don't lose any data.

## Nonprofit discount

Registered nonprofits get 30% off any plan. Email support@fernbill.example with proof of registration to apply.
