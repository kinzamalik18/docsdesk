# Security and two-factor authentication

## Two-factor authentication

Turn on two-factor authentication in **Profile > Security**. Fernbill supports authenticator apps such as Google Authenticator, 1Password and Authy. SMS codes are not supported.

When you turn it on, save your 10 backup codes somewhere safe. Each code works once. If you lose your phone and your backup codes, the account owner must contact support to verify identity before 2FA can be reset. This takes up to 3 business days.

Business plan owners can require two-factor authentication for every team member.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 12 characters. Fernbill checks new passwords against lists of known leaked passwords and rejects them.

## How your data is protected

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest with AES-256. Card details are handled by Stripe or PayPal and never stored on Fernbill's servers. Fernbill's servers are located in the European Union.

## Report a security issue

Email security@fernbill.example. Please don't report security issues through the normal support address.
